## Strategy selected

No legitimately compatible public animal/farm-level dataset with matching fields, authorization, licensing clarity, and Indian dairy representativeness was established. The committed training generator therefore creates 800 deterministic synthetic feature vectors with Poisson/binomial/gamma-shaped technical distributions and seed 25007. It generates no drug dose, withdrawal period, MRL, diagnosis, safety outcome, or misuse label. Controlled high-frequency vectors exist only in tests to verify ranking behavior.

Before any real deployment, a domain expert must approve feature semantics; the platform operator must define consent, retention, representativeness, and drift monitoring; and the model must be retrained/evaluated on sufficiently broad authorized application data.
